# Rohan (Legal) — DPIIT / Startup India Recognition: Checklist + Draft Declarations

Grounded in the current DPIIT framework per Gazette Notification G.S.R. 108(E) (Feb 2026 revision). Confirm exact thresholds on startupindia.gov.in at the time of filing — this framework gets revised periodically and I'm working from the most recent version on file, not guaranteeing it hasn't shifted again since.

## The one thing to do first

**Nothing below can be filed yet — SteadyNest isn't incorporated.** DPIIT recognition requires an existing legal entity (Private Limited Company, LLP, Partnership Firm, or the new Cooperative Society category). Incorporate first. Once the Certificate of Incorporation (COI) exists, everything in this checklist becomes actionable the same day — there's no waiting period required between incorporation and applying for recognition.

Given SteadyNest needs Section 80-IAC eligibility and will likely raise outside capital eventually, **incorporate as a Private Limited Company**, not an LLP — most investors and the 80-IAC tax holiday assume a Pvt Ltd structure.

## Eligibility checklist (confirm each before filing)

- [ ] Entity incorporated as a Private Limited Company (or LLP/Partnership/Cooperative Society), and it exists — no filing before this.
- [ ] Incorporated within the last 10 years as of the application date.
- [ ] Annual turnover under ₹200 crore in any financial year since incorporation (doubled from ₹100 crore under the 2026 revision — SteadyNest, pre-revenue, clears this trivially).
- [ ] Entity was not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an already-existing business.
- [ ] Can articulate innovation/scalability in the write-up — SteadyNest's angle: geo-search property discovery + tokenized autopay + social/safety layer bundled into one Delhi-NCR-first product, not a single feature copy of an existing app.
- [ ] Not pursuing the new "Deep Tech Startup" category (20-year window, ₹300 crore ceiling) — that doesn't fit SteadyNest's profile; file under the general category.

## How to apply, in order

1. **Incorporate** (see `incorporation-playbook.md` for the SPICe+ walkthrough — Part A/B, DSC, DIN, e-MOA/e-AOA, AGILE-PRO-S). This step is the actual blocker right now.
2. **Register on startupindia.gov.in.** Submit: company details (CIN, incorporation date), an innovation/business-model write-up (draft below), and supporting docs — Certificate of Incorporation is mandatory; a pitch deck is optional but strengthens the write-up.
3. **Wait for recognition** — typically granted within a couple of weeks, no physical inspection involved.
4. **Only after recognition, if pursuing the tax holiday:** file a separate Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) application through the same portal, with financials and a more detailed business plan. This is a distinct step from recognition itself — don't conflate the two or expect 80-IAC automatically once recognized.

## What recognition unlocks (and what to actually chase now vs. later)

| Benefit | Chase now? |
|---|---|
| Section 80-IAC — 100% tax deduction for any 3 consecutive FYs within the first 10 years | Later. Needs a separate IMB filing with real financials; SteadyNest is pre-revenue, so there's nothing to shelter yet. Revisit once there's a P&L worth protecting. |
| 80% patent filing fee rebate, 50% trademark filing fee rebate | Now, opportunistically — if trademark filing for "SteadyNest"/"Ram OS" happens post-incorporation, the rebate applies automatically once recognized; no reason to leave it unclaimed. |
| Self-certification under labour/environment laws | Now — see `compliance-checklist.md` for what this actually covers day-to-day. |
| Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) — up to ₹20 lakh milestone grant, or up to ₹50 lakh via convertible debt through an approved incubator | Time-boxed: eligibility requires incorporation ≤2 years before the SISFS application. Don't let recognition itself slip past that window even if the grant isn't the immediate priority — the 2-year clock is the reason to get recognition done promptly after incorporating, not to delay it. |

**Bottom line: get DPIIT recognition immediately after incorporation — it's free, has no downside, and starts the clock on SISFS eligibility. Save 80-IAC and the IMB filing for when there's an actual financial story to tell.**

### 1. Declaration of Non-Splitting

> I, [Founder Name], sole director of [SteadyNest Pvt Ltd / entity name], hereby declare that the said entity has been incorporated as a new and distinct legal entity, and has not been formed by the splitting up or reconstruction of an already existing business. The entity represents an original venture built to develop and operate the SteadyNest rental and tenant-safety mobile application for the Delhi NCR market.

### 2. Declaration of Turnover

> I, [Founder Name], hereby declare that [SteadyNest Pvt Ltd] has not, in any financial year since its incorporation, exceeded an annual turnover of ₹200 crore (Rupees Two Hundred Crore), in accordance with the eligibility criteria under the Startup India initiative (Gazette Notification G.S.R. 108(E)). [As a pre-revenue entity at the time of this application, turnover to date is nil / negligible.]

### 3. Innovation & Scalability Write-Up (for the recognition application)

> SteadyNest is a mobile application addressing the fragmented rental experience for tenants and landlords in Delhi NCR. It combines geolocation-based property discovery (using PostGIS-backed geo-search with an adjustable search radius), tokenized rent autopay to remove manual bank transfers, and a social/safety layer — including swipe-based local matching and an emergency SOS feature — that addresses the specific needs of tenants who are new to the city. No existing platform in the Indian PropTech space combines transactional rent management with a social/safety layer in a single product; this bundling, not any single feature in isolation, is SteadyNest's core innovation. The product is built and iterated by a solo, AI-assisted founder, with a phased Delhi-NCR-first launch strategy designed for capital-efficient scaling into other NCR-adjacent metros post product-market fit.
