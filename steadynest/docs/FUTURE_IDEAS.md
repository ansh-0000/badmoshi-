# Future Ideas Backlog

These are ideas for future versions, specifically geared toward making the app a top-notch travel companion for users moving city-to-city or country-to-country. **Do not build these now; focus on stabilizing the MVP.**

### A. Advanced Social Discovery (The "Connect" Feature Evolution)
*   **Travel Itinerary Overlap:** Allow users to input future travel dates and destinations. Match them with people who will be in the *same city at the same time* for meetups, co-working, or flat-sharing.
*   **Local Communities & Micro-Events:** "Squads" or temporary groups for expats/travelers in a specific city to organize dinners, hikes, or language exchange meetups.

### B. "Life Admin" & Logistics Management
*   **Visa & Entry Requirement Tracker:** An automated hub where users input passport nationality and destination, providing up-to-date visa requirements and digital nomad visa applications.
*   **Cost of Living & Currency Intelligence:** A dashboard comparing home currency to destination, providing real-time estimates for rent, coffee, internet, etc.
*   **Expense Splitting:** A built-in ledger for matched roommates/travel buddies to split costs.

### C. Hyper-Local Tira AI Concierge
*   **Cultural Etiquette & Tipping Engine:** Tira AI proactively notifies users upon arrival about local customs (e.g., tipping rules, greetings).
*   **Transit & Neighborhood Safety AI:** Tira analyzes a property's neighborhood and provides safety/walkability scores and transit advice.

### D. Upgraded Emergency SOS & Health
*   **Dynamic Emergency Contacts:** Automatically detect the user's current country and update local Police, Ambulance, and Fire numbers (e.g., 911, 112, 100).
*   **Nearby Verified Clinics:** A map layer showing English-speaking (or user's native language) clinics in foreign cities.

### E. The "Offline-First" Promise
*   **Downloadable City Packs:** Allow downloading a city's rental map, saved profiles, offline Tira AI chat, and SOS module before boarding a flight.
