SteadyNest · Ram OS · Missing screens · Deliverable 2
The half of the app that isn't drawn yet
Twenty screens across landlord mode, onboarding, payments, identity and system states — same frame, same palette, same type rules as the fourteen tenant screens already built. Every screen with real data behind it carries its loading and empty state on a switcher above the phone, so nothing ships without them.
HOW TO READ IT
Click the pills above any phone to switch between populated, loading and empty. They are live, not screenshots.
TYPE RULES HELD
Playfair for names and headline numbers. Mono for every rupee figure, date, time and coordinate. Inter for everything else.
DECISIONS I MADE
11 months is the autopay default. Landlord money is Moss, money at risk is Rust. Role choice comes straight after OTP and is reversible in Profile.
STILL NEEDED FROM YOU
Real listing photography, and confirmation of which languages ship first — the translator list below is my guess at the priority order.
