# Priya (Marketing) — 4-Week Delhi NCR Content Calendar

**Scope decision, stated up front:** SteadyNest isn't publicly launched and isn't incorporated yet, so this calendar is a **pre-launch waitlist/awareness push**, not a "download now" campaign — no App Store/Play Store links, no claims that require an entity behind them (no "we guarantee," no payment-processing claims). It's also scoped to what's *actually working* today per the product's real feature state: property discovery, the "what's new around here" local-spots map, and roommate/co-living swipe-matching are all "working at a basic level" — lead with these. Autopay, real-time chat, the AI voice translator, Tira AI, and Emergency SOS are all still being debugged per the founder's own tracking, so this calendar does **not** promise them as live, working features — mentioning them at all is framed as "coming soon," never as something a new user can rely on today. Overpromising a feature that's still buggy is the fastest way to burn early trust with the first Delhi NCR users who try it.

**Channels:** Instagram + LinkedIn (zero-budget, organic). Push to Sheets/Canva is blocked — Drive and Canva connectors aren't authorized yet. Copy below is ready to paste manually until that's connected.

## Content pillars

1. **"New in Delhi, not new to this"** — speaks to tenants relocating to Delhi NCR who need housing + orientation fast.
2. **Property discovery, made visual** — the map + radius slider is the most demo-able working feature; show, don't tell.
3. **Founder-building-in-public** — solo, AI-assisted build story; resonates on LinkedIn with other builders and lends authenticity pre-launch.
4. **Roommate/co-living matching** — the swipe-based matching feature, positioned for students/young professionals moving to Delhi NCR alone.

## What's explicitly *not* in this calendar, and why

- **No autopay/payment messaging** — feature is still being debugged; promising "seamless rent payments" before it's reliable risks a bad first impression and, if it touches money, real user harm if it fails.
- **No SOS/safety-feature promotion yet** — this is the highest-stakes feature in the product and per its own design review (see `steadynest-design-system.md`) doesn't yet have full state feedback. Marketing a safety feature before it's hardened is a trust risk worth avoiding, not just a product one.
- **No "download now" CTA** — no public app store listing yet; every CTA above routes to a waitlist, not a store link.
